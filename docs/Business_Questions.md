Answering these business questions will enable stakeholders to:

- Identify churn drivers.
- Improve product adoption.
- Optimize marketing campaigns.
- Enhance customer support.
- Increase customer retention.
- Make data-driven business decisions.

These questions will serve as the foundation for SQL analysis, Python EDA, Power BI dashboards, and executive reporting throughout the project.
