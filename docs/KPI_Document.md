# Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used to measure business performance throughout the project. These KPIs will be calculated using SQL, analyzed in Python, and visualized in Power BI dashboards.

---

# KPI Categories

1. Executive KPIs
2. Customer KPIs
3. Revenue KPIs
4. Subscription KPIs
5. Product Usage KPIs
6. Support KPIs
7. Marketing KPIs
8. Retention KPIs

---

# 1. Executive KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Customers | Total number of registered customer accounts |
| Active Customers | Customers with active subscriptions |
| Trial Customers | Customers currently using a trial plan |
| Churned Customers | Customers who have churned |
| Customer Growth Rate | Percentage increase in customers over time |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | Total recurring monthly subscription revenue |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | Annualized recurring revenue |
| Overall Churn Rate | Percentage of customers who churned |
| Overall Retention Rate | Percentage of customers retained |

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Accurately calculates all defined KPIs.
- Presents KPIs in interactive Power BI dashboards.

- Supports strategic decision-making through clear visualizations and business insights.
- Demonstrates industry-standard analytics practices suitable for a professional portfolio.
